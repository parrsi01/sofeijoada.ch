## Menu page artwork

The separately supplied `F67478D1-2C00-44F3-B532-3A411CA34F54.PNG` is retained as `menu-source.png` (850 × 1851). Its black screenshot margins are excluded from the displayed artwork: rows 285 through 1561 become `menu-poster.png` and its lossless WebP counterpart (850 × 1277). The design, dessert photos, wording, and prices are preserved. The supplied image advertises 1 Feijoada + 1 dessert for 27.–, 2 Feijoada + 1 dessert for 37.–, and 2 Feijoada + 2 desserts for 44.–. The dessert names are transcribed exactly from the image.

Only one menu language image was supplied, so every Menu tab displays that French artwork. Page controls and readable bundle labels use the selected language. The original 11 posters remain on the Home tab.

The readable menu transcribes the supplied descriptions and prices, with separate translations for website controls. The Russian artwork does not advertise free delivery, and the Romanian artwork does not show a pickup label; the corresponding readable menus preserve those differences. Prices remain in the artwork's `20.–` / `30.–` notation.

`whatsapp-qr.png`, `facade.png`, and `sm-marketing-logo.png` preserve the supplied INFO files. The facade and marketing logo are retained as source assets. The QR portion was cropped to `whatsapp-code.webp` without resizing; it is used in the expandable ordering section.

Website ordering links go directly to that WhatsApp contact rather than depending on the QR redirect service. The displayed phone number is the one on the posters, `076.210.70.87`, linked in international format as `tel:+41762107087`.

No remote fonts, embedded social widgets, analytics, or tracking pixels are loaded. WhatsApp is contacted only when a visitor follows an ordering link or scans the QR with their own device. Opening an external link uses `noopener noreferrer`.
